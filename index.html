Yerevan Commute Map

This map runs on Yandex Maps, which needs your free Yandex Maps API key. The key is saved only in this browser.

  1. Open developer.tech.yandex.ru and sign in with your Yandex account.
  2. Choose Connect APIs, then JavaScript API and HTTP Geocoder, and fill in the short form.
  3. Copy the key it shows. A new key can take up to 15 minutes to start working.

Bus stops

Train times are South Caucasus Railway's official timetable. Track shapes come from the Natural Earth railway dataset, adjusted to pass through each station. They are simplified, so they can sit a few hundred metres off the real track between stations.

Bus paths are drawn by Yandex along real roads through each route's main stops. Yandex plots them as car routes, so a bus may take a slightly different street. If Yandex can't plot a route, it shows dashed, and the line under the route says why.
